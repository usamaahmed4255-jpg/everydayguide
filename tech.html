Tech How-Tos

Phones, computers, Wi-Fi, apps, passwords, and everyday software — fixed in plain steps.

Tech problems are usually smaller than they feel. A slow laptop, a Wi-Fi connection that drops, a phone that is out of space, a password you cannot remember — each one has a cause, and each cause has a fix you can work through in order. These guides assume no technical background. They say exactly where to click, what to look for, and what to try next if the first step does not work. They also say when to stop: some jobs, like a swollen battery or a damaged charger, are not DIY jobs at all. Menus and settings change as software updates, so guides are corrected when a step goes out of date, and readers can report a changed menu through the Contact page.
