Money Basics

Budgeting, saving, credit, and the everyday money decisions that come up in real life.

Money basics are not about getting rich. They are about the decisions that come up every month: building a budget you will actually keep, saving a first emergency fund on a small income, understanding how a credit score is built, and setting a savings target that survives contact with real life. These guides use plain numbers and worked examples, and they flag the US/UK differences where they genuinely matter, such as credit scoring and tax-year rules. Nothing here is financial advice — every money guide says so, and the Disclaimer page explains why. For advice about your own situation, speak to a qualified adviser or a free debt-advice service.
