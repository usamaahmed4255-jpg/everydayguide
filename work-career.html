Work & Career

CVs and résumés, cover letters, interviews, remote work, and workplace skills.

Work rewards people who can show what they can do, clearly and briefly. That is harder than it sounds when you have no experience yet, are changing direction, or are setting up to work from home for the first time. These guides cover the documents and moments that decide that: CVs with no experience, cover letters for a first job, the interview questions that trip people up, and a home office that does not take over your life. You will find template outlines and worked examples, not just advice to “be confident”. The focus is on what employers and interviewers actually check, and on honest, practical steps you can take this week.
