About The Everyday Guide

The Everyday Guide is a small, independent how-to site. We write clear, practical guides for everyday problems — the kind of thing you search for when something is not working, you are starting something new, or you just want to do a job properly the first time.

What we are not

We are not a news site, not a review site, and not a place for hot takes. We do not publish sponsored guides, we do not sell rankings, and we do not recommend products we have not looked into. If a guide mentions a product or service by name, it is because it is genuinely useful for the task — not because anyone paid for the mention.

Who runs this site

The Everyday Guide is run by its editor, Jordan Ellis.

Jordan writes practical, step-by-step guides for everyday problems — tech, work, money, and home. Every guide is checked before publishing, and readers’ corrections keep them up to date.
